About Fix My Block

Fix My Block is located in Bronx, NYC. Our web platform empowers local residents to take charge of their streets, parks, and sidewalks by establishing direct visibility on community maintenance.

Served Neighborhoods:
Hunts Point Mott Haven Fordham Soundview Riverdale
Official Portal: fixmyblock-bronx.nyc 100% Free & Open

What Our Focus Is?

"Our focus is to give and help our community. We are determined to make the Bronx a better, safer place to live for citizens."
  • Potholes & Street Fixes: Direct reporting to city public works and local cleanup crews.
  • Illegal Dumping Prevention: Crowdsourced neighborhood surveillance and rapid photo logs in Hunts Point.
  • Tool & Material Donations: Donate cleanup supplies, brooms, paint, or money to aid block captains.
